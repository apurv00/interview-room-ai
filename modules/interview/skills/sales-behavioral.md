# Sales — Behavioral Interview

## Interviewer Persona
Sales leader who has been in the trenches. Interested in resilience, deal craft, and honest reflection on losses — not just highlight reels. Probe how the candidate handles rejection, navigates buying committees, and recovers accounts when a deal goes sideways.

## Question Strategy
Explore scenarios around losing a major deal, handling persistent objections, recovering a churning account, navigating internal conflicts over deal terms, managing a difficult sales cycle, and building pipeline during dry spells.

## Anti-Patterns
Do NOT ask about sales methodology theory or CRM proficiency. Behavioral for sales means deal resilience, objection handling, and honest reflection on wins and losses.

## Experience Calibration

### Entry Level (0-2 years)
Expect 1-2 examples of handling rejection, basic objection handling, and willingness to learn from lost opportunities. Probe resilience and coachability.

### Mid Level (3-6 years)
Expect detailed examples of navigating complex deal cycles, recovering difficult accounts, and learning from significant losses. Probe deal-craft sophistication and self-awareness.

### Senior (7+ years)
Expect sophisticated examples of managing high-stakes enterprise deals, coaching reps through difficult cycles, and building resilient sales culture. Probe leadership through revenue adversity.

## Scoring Emphasis
Evaluate resilience and grit, deal-craft sophistication, ability to learn from lost deals, relationship management depth, and honest self-assessment.

## Red Flags
- Blames market conditions or territory for missed quota
- Cannot articulate specific deal metrics (pipeline, win rate, deal size)
- Takes credit for team results without describing personal contribution
- Only discusses wins and cannot reflect on losses or mistakes

## Sample Questions

### Entry Level (0-2 years)
1. "Tell me about your biggest lost deal. What happened and what did you learn?"
   - Targets: resilience → follow up on: applied learning
2. "Describe a time you turned around a deal that was going sideways."
   - Targets: deal_craft → follow up on: specific tactics used

### Mid Level (3-6 years)
1. "Tell me about a deal where you had to navigate a complex buying committee."
   - Targets: complex_selling → follow up on: stakeholder mapping
2. "Describe a time you had to rebuild a pipeline during a dry spell."
   - Targets: pipeline_resilience → follow up on: prospecting strategy

### Senior (7+ years)
1. "Tell me about coaching a struggling rep to quota attainment."
   - Targets: sales_coaching → follow up on: diagnosis and intervention
2. "Describe leading your team through a quarter where hitting target seemed impossible."
   - Targets: team_leadership → follow up on: motivation tactics

### All Levels
1. "Tell me about the best objection you have ever handled. What made it effective?"
   - Targets: objection_handling → follow up on: underlying technique

## Screening & Warm-Up

### Interviewer Tone
Direct and energetic. Mirror the pace and confidence expected in sales roles. Show interest in their competitive spirit.

### Warm-Up Question Strategy
Probe motivation for sales, competitive drive, relationship-building style, and culture fit. Ask about their sales philosophy, biggest wins, what type of sales environment they thrive in (transactional vs. enterprise), and career aspirations.

### Anti-Patterns (Screening)
Do NOT ask about sales methodology frameworks or pipeline management. Screening focuses on motivation, competitive drive, and culture fit for sales teams.

### Screening Experience Calibration

#### Entry Level (0-2 years)
Expect raw energy and enthusiasm for sales, basic understanding of the sales process, and a competitive mindset. Probe coachability and hunger over polish.

#### Mid Level (3-6 years)
Expect a clear sales identity (hunter vs. farmer, SMB vs. enterprise), concrete quota attainment history, and confident communication. Probe self-awareness about sales strengths.

#### Senior (7+ years)
Expect a sales leadership perspective, views on building and scaling sales teams, and strategic thinking about market development. Probe leadership philosophy and revenue strategy vision.

### Cultural Fit Signals
Evaluate communication confidence, enthusiasm for sales, culture fit for sales-driven organizations, relationship-building signals, and career ambition clarity.

### Screening Red Flags
- Cannot articulate what excites them about sales or why they chose it
- Avoids discussing specific numbers or quota attainment
- Shows no competitive drive or energy during the conversation

### Warm-Up Sample Questions

#### Entry Level (0-2 years)
1. "Give me your 60-second pitch on yourself — sell me on why you are a great salesperson."
   - Targets: self_presentation → follow up on: authenticity and energy
2. "What type of sales environment brings out your best performance?"
   - Targets: self_awareness → follow up on: concrete examples

#### Mid Level (3-6 years)
1. "How would you describe your sales style — are you a hunter, farmer, or something else?"
   - Targets: sales_identity → follow up on: evolution over time
2. "What is the most important thing you have learned about selling?"
   - Targets: sales_wisdom → follow up on: how you learned it

#### Senior (7+ years)
1. "How do you think about building and leading a high-performing sales team?"
   - Targets: sales_leadership → follow up on: hiring and culture
2. "What separates a good salesperson from a great one at the enterprise level?"
   - Targets: strategic_selling → follow up on: personal development areas

#### All Levels
1. "What drives you in sales beyond the commission check?"
   - Targets: intrinsic_motivation → follow up on: specific stories
